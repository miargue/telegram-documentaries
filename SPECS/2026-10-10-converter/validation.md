## Deviations recorded at verification

1. **`get_converter_timeout` is stricter than the plan.** `plan.md` §6 only
   specified "blank falls back to `120.0`". The shipped `config.py` also raises
   `ConfigError` for a present-but-unparsable value *and* for a value that
   parses but cannot be a real timeout (non-finite `nan`/`inf`, or `<= 0`), so a
   typo (e.g. `GEMINI_CONVERTER_TIMEOUT=abc`, `-1`, `inf`) fails loudly at
   startup instead of silently disarming the deadline. Covered by
   `test_config.py::test_get_converter_timeout_rejects_a_non_numeric_value`,
   `test_get_converter_timeout_rejects_a_non_positive_value`,
   `test_get_converter_timeout_rejects_a_non_finite_value`. Benign and
   documented in `README.md`; no other drift. **User approval requested.**

2. **No other drift found.** The shipped surface (module list, port/adapters,
   events, session v3, routing, config keys and defaults) matches
   `requirements.md` and `plan.md`. `SPECS/MISSION.md` needs no change.
